Hi! I am `Chen Chu` (初晨), I am a second-year Computer Science Ph.D. student at the University of Southern California(USC), advised by Professor [Cyrus Shahabi](https://infolab.usc.edu/). I am passionate about developing SOTA AI models to solve geospatial problems, which involved spatial understanding of LLMs, spatial representation learning. My most recent research interests include **Spatial Reasoning of LLMs**, **Multimodal LLMs**, **Spatially Representation Learning**, **Generative AI**. 

Prior to my Ph.D., I earned my Master degree from State Key Laboratory of Resources and Environmental Information System(LREIS), University of Chinese Academy of Sciences(UCAS), where I was supervised by Professor [Feng Lu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=oeS87xoAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN) and Associate Professor [Hengcai Zhang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ys6nhTMAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN&oi=ao).
